# Tracking — External Sources

*The user-facing channel for tracking ideas that aren't ready for evaluation yet:
YouTube recommendations, screener dashboards, sell-side desks, news articles,
podcasts, manual research notes — anything that surfaces a name worth keeping
radar eyes on without committing to a gate-card evaluation.*

## Why a tracking tier

The watchlist (`input/watchlists/*.md`) is for candidates being actively evaluated.
The radar feeds the daily evaluation only on holdings + watchlist names, so adding
a name to a watchlist means committing to running its full gate-card stack on every
run. That's expensive (gates 1-2 need the scorer; 5-7 need live web) and noisy if
the name doesn't really warrant attention yet. Tracking is the holding pen for
ideas that aren't quiet enough to subscribe to silently.

## How to add a name

Append a row to the table at the bottom of this file. Three columns: Ticker, Source, Notes.

Sources you might use:

- **YouTube**: channel or video title + date. "YouTube — mega-cap AI infra 12 Aug 26".
- **Screener / dashboard**: the tool and when you read it. "Screener dashboard 14 Aug 26".
- **News article**: publication + date. "FT — rare-earth supply 14 Aug 26".
- **Sell-side desk / analyst**: name + date. "Berenberg — note 14 Aug 26".
- **Personal**: free-form. "Watching pre-print 14 Aug 26".

Keep the source generic enough to publish. What matters to the radar is the
*date* and that a human can retrace the idea — not which subscription it came
from.

Run `python3 engine/heartbeat_radar.py` after editing. The ticker will screen
in the next radar and aggregate into the sector rotation read.

**There is no per-sector cap on this file.** The old cap of 8 was scaffolding
for the count-based rotation read and was retired with it — bellwether ETFs
supply sector direction now (`docs/BACKLOG.md` item 23). Add freely; prune stale
rows rather than displacing good ones. The only sizing rule left is a *floor*,
and it lives in `sector-coverage.md`: a sector with fewer than three screened
members needs its bellwether to confirm before its own members can carry an
exit read.

**Column 2 is the sector, not free text.** `engine/heartbeat_radar.py` reads the
second cell of every tracking row as the ticker's sector (`@Defence` and `Defence`
both parse). `sector_map.md` still wins where it carries the ticker — which it does
for every row here — but a name absent from the map takes this cell verbatim, so a
source string in column 2 would file it under a sector called "YouTube video 17 Aug
26". The table was `| Ticker | Source | Notes |` until 2026-08-23 and read exactly
that way; it is now `| Ticker | Sector | Source | Notes |`, matching what the engine
expects and what `sector-coverage.md` has always used.

**Demoting from a watchlist is a legitimate use of this file.** A name that has
produced `AVOID` every run for months is paying a full gate-card cost to reach
"no". Moved here it keeps everything the radar gives it — price, 150d, trend,
breakout, RS percentile, flags, and its vote in the sector rotation read — and
stops consuming an evaluation slot. What it loses is the forced daily verdict:
nothing makes anyone look at its radar row. Demote names you understand and
would not buy; keep anything you would act on within a week.

## When the radar flags something interesting

Check `output/radar/latest.md`. If the flag reads as a real candidate,
**promote** to a watchlist (move the row to `input/watchlists/<your list>.md`,
delete the tracking row). If not, leave the row alone or drop the row entirely
after several sweeps of no follow-through.
